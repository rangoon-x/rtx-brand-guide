About Rangoon.Tech

Short (one line)

Rangoon.Tech is a Yangon-based experiential technology partner that builds interactive experiences for brands and agencies.

Long (one paragraph)

Founded in 2019 in Yangon, Myanmar, Rangoon.Tech is an experiential technology partner combining creativity, software and hardware to build practical, innovative and reliable customer experiences. The team creates AR mirrors, TikTok effects and games, interactive microsites, print and packaging AR, virtual try-on, event tools and sales promotions for brands and agencies. Logo rules

Do

  • Use the primary logo whenever possible.
  • Use the Burst Mark for icons and small spaces.
  • Keep clear space around the logo.
  • Use the white logo on dark or busy backgrounds.

Don't

  • Stretch, rotate, recolour or rearrange the logo.
  • Add shadows, outlines or other effects.
  • Retype the logo in a font. Use the files above.
